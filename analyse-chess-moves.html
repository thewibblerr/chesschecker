How to analyse a chess screenshot

  1. Take or save a screenshot showing the full chessboard.
  2. Open Chess Checker and upload the screenshot or paste it from the clipboard.
  3. Chess Checker recognises the pieces and creates a FEN position.
  4. Check the confidence score. If anything looks wrong, open the board editor and correct the affected square.
  5. Confirm which side is to move, then choose Analyse on Lichess or Analyse on Coachess.

What Chess Checker does

Chess Checker is a screenshot-to-FEN tool built to remove the fiddly part of recreating a chess position by hand. It recognises a board from an image, estimates board orientation and side to move, produces the FEN notation used by chess analysis tools, and gives you a visual editor for correcting any piece that was misread.

It can also identify a number of common openings and save recent positions locally on your device. Once a position is ready, you can send it directly to established chess analysis services rather than loading a large chess engine inside the website.

What is FEN?

FEN, or Forsyth-Edwards Notation, is a compact text format for describing a chess position. Chess analysis websites use it to recreate the exact arrangement of pieces and the side to move. Chess Checker creates the FEN automatically from your screenshot and copies it for you.
